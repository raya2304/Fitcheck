BMI GUIDE

Understanding BMI

BMI is commonly divided into four general categories for adults.

01

Underweight

Below 18.5

A BMI below 18.5 is generally classified as underweight for adults.

02

Healthy Weight

18.5 – 24.9

This is the commonly used healthy BMI range for adults.

03

Overweight

25 – 29.9

A BMI from 25 to 29.9 is generally classified as overweight.

04

Obesity

30 or higher

A BMI of 30 or higher falls within the obesity category.

WHY BMI?

A Simple Health Screening Tool

BMI uses your height and weight to produce a numerical value. It can be useful as a general screening measure, but it does not provide a complete picture of individual health.

📏

Height

Your height is used as part of the BMI calculation.

⚖️

Weight

Your body weight is combined with your height.

📊

BMI Number

The calculation produces your BMI value.

ℹ️

Important Information

BMI is a screening measure and does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass or overall health. It should not be used as a medical diagnosis. Individual health can depend on many different factors.
